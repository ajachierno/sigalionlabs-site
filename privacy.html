Mystiko

Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Mystiko is an encrypted messaging app for Android, published by Sigalion Labs, the app studio of Roo Management, Inc. ("we"). This policy covers the Mystiko app, the server that passes messages between phones (the "relay"), and this website.

  • Your messages are encrypted on your phone and can only be opened on the phone you sent them to. We cannot read them.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address or contacts.
  • We show no ads, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

What we cannot see

  • The content of your messages.
  • Who sent a message. The sender's identity is inside the encrypted message.
  • How long the sender chose to keep a message.
  • The names you give your contacts, your chat history, or your encryption level. These stay on your phone.

What our relay stores

  • Your public keys and Mystiko ID. These are created on your phone when you first open the app. Other people's phones need them to encrypt messages to you. They are not secret, and the ID is derived from the keys. Your private keys never leave your phone.
  • Your stack of single-use public keys. Your phone uploads these so that each message sent to you can be sealed to a key that is used once. The relay hands each one to a single sender and then deletes it. The matching private keys stay on your phone and are destroyed after use.
  • Encrypted messages waiting for delivery. This includes any photo or file sent with a message, which is encrypted in exactly the same way. A message addressed to you is held until your phone collects it, and then it is deleted from the relay. A message nobody collects is deleted after 30 days. While it waits, it exists only in encrypted form.

The relay keeps no record of who has talked to whom, and it does not log the requests it receives. The server's error log can include the IP address of a connection that failed, and that log is kept for no more than 14 days.

Network information

Any device that connects to a server on the internet reveals its IP address to that server's hosting and network providers. The relay runs on a server rented from DigitalOcean in the United States. We do not use IP addresses to identify you or to build a profile, and the relay does not attach them to your Mystiko ID.

Direct links between phones

For each chat you choose how your messages travel: through the relay, or over a direct link from your phone to the other person's. The relay is the setting until you change it, and the app never changes it for you. If a direct message cannot be delivered it is not sent, and you decide what happens to it.

  • What a direct link reveals. To connect, each phone learns the other's IP address, which can show a rough location and network provider. It also shows the other person that you have the app open at that moment. Choose a direct link only with people you are comfortable sharing that with.
  • When your phone answers. Only while Mystiko is open, and only for a contact whose chat you have set to direct. You can choose to answer every contact you have not blocked. Otherwise your phone stays silent, which looks the same as being offline.
  • What we see. The invitation that sets up a link passes through the relay encrypted like any message, so we cannot read it. To find a path between the two phones, each one asks a helper on our server what its public address is. That helper sees the IP address that asked and nothing else, and it keeps no log. Messages sent phone to phone never reach our servers, and no copy is held anywhere between the two phones.
  • The pass-through helper. Some networks, mobile networks especially, do not let two phones connect to each other. When that happens, a direct link can go through a helper on our server instead. The helper forwards the encrypted traffic as it passes and cannot read it. It stores nothing and keeps no log. Like any server, it sees the IP addresses of the two phones while the link is open. You can turn the helper off in the app, in which case a direct link is made phone to phone or not at all, and the chat always shows which kind of link you have.
  • Holding a message. If a direct link is not available, you can keep a message on your own phone until it is. It stays encrypted on your phone and goes nowhere until the link opens or you choose another route.

What stays on your phone

  • Your private keys, stored encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore.
  • Your contacts' names as you typed them, your messages, and your settings, all stored encrypted on the device.

The app turns off Android's cloud backup for this data, because a backup could not be decrypted on another device. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, this data is gone and we cannot recover it.

How long messages last

The sender of each message chooses how long it is kept: until it has been read, for 5 days, for 30 days, or with no limit. The sender can also delete a message they sent. These choices are carried out by the app on each phone. They cannot stop a recipient from copying a message by other means, such as photographing the screen.

Inviting someone by text message

If you choose "Invite by text", Android's contact picker lets you select one person, and the app receives that one phone number so it can open your texting app with an invitation already written. You send the text yourself. Mystiko does not read your contact list and does not send the number to us.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card details. Google tells the app which subscription is active so the app can apply your encryption level. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Notifications

The app checks for new messages in the background and can show a notification saying that a message has arrived. The notification never includes the sender or the content.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: to reach the relay and, if you choose a direct link, the other phone or the pass-through helper.
  • Notifications: to tell you a message has arrived.

The app does not request access to your contacts, text messages, location, camera, microphone or files. To send a photo or file you choose it in Android's own picker, which hands the app that one item and nothing else. A photo is shrunk and saved again before it is sent, which removes hidden details such as where it was taken. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Mystiko deletes your keys, contacts and messages from your phone.

To have your public keys and any waiting messages removed from the relay, email support@sigalionlabs.com with your Mystiko ID, which is shown in the app under the person icon. We hold no name, email address or phone number for you, so the ID is the only way we can find your record. We will delete it and confirm.

This website

This site sets no cookies and includes no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. It is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, processes the IP address of a device requesting a page.

Children

Mystiko is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Requests from authorities

If we are legally required to hand over data, the only data we hold is what is listed under "What our relay stores": public keys, Mystiko IDs, and encrypted messages not yet delivered. Once a message has been read, the key that could open it no longer exists anywhere. We do not hold the keys that would decrypt those messages.
