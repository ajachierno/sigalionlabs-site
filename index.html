02 Closed circuit

Mystiko speaks only to Mystiko

No phone number. No email address. No contact list. You are a 26-character ID, and you hand it to the people you choose.

  • Sealed in your hand

    A message is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, and opened only on the phone it was sent to.

  • A new key every time

    Each message gets its own key. Nothing is reused from one message to the next.

  • Sender unknown

    Your identity travels inside the sealed message. Our server passes it along without learning who wrote it.

  • Nothing for us to read

    We hold no key that opens your messages. If we are asked for them, sealed text is all there is.

03 The sender decides

Your words, your clock

Whoever writes a message chooses how long it lives, and the choice holds on both phones.

  1. Once

    Delete after reading

    It stays closed until the reader taps it, then it is gone when they leave the chat.

  2. 5d

    Five days

    Removed from both phones five days after you sent it.

  3. 30d

    Thirty days

    Removed from both phones thirty days after you sent it.

  4. ∞

    No limit

    Kept until one of you deletes it.

Take it back. Press and hold a message you sent and it is removed from both phones. Screenshots are blocked inside the app. A screen can still be photographed with another camera, and no app can prevent that.

05 In transit

What crosses the wire

Your phone

Our server

Their phone

Between the two phones there is only sealed text. This is an illustration, and the real thing looks much the same to anyone watching.

  • What our server holds

    Your public keys, and sealed messages that have not been collected yet. Once a phone collects a message, it is deleted from the server.

  • What it never has

    Your private keys, your contacts' names, your chat history, or the lifespan you chose for a message. Those stay on your phone.
